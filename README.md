# MUSIQ AI – AI-Powered Multilingual YouTube Music Assistant

MUSIQ AI is an intelligent, Python-based Command-Line Interface (CLI) application that brings conversational Artificial Intelligence to YouTube Music. It transforms traditional music discovery into an interactive experience by allowing users to search, discover, and interact with music using natural-language commands in multiple languages.

Instead of relying on manual searches or predefined commands, users can simply describe what they want to listen to. The AI assistant understands their intent, preferences, mood, and activities to provide relevant music suggestions and a more personalized discovery experience.

## Key Features

- **Multilingual AI Interaction:** Communicate with the assistant in multiple languages, including support for mixed-language conversations.
- **Natural Language Understanding:** Understands everyday conversational requests instead of requiring specific commands.
- **Mood-Based Music Discovery:** Discover music based on moods such as happiness, relaxation, motivation, and focus.
- **Activity-Based Recommendations:** Find suitable music for studying, coding, working out, travelling, and other activities.
- **Intelligent Music Search:** Search for songs, artists, albums, and playlists through conversational requests.
- **AI-Powered Recommendations:** Get music suggestions based on user preferences and interests.
- **Conversational Context:** Supports follow-up requests to refine music searches and recommendations.
- **Command-Line Experience:** A lightweight and interactive terminal-based interface powered by Python.

## Why MUSIQ AI?

Traditional music discovery often requires users to search manually using song titles, artists, or genres. MUSIQ AI introduces a more natural approach where users can express their musical preferences in everyday language.

The project aims to make music discovery more accessible, personalized, and convenient, particularly for users who prefer regional languages or want music recommendations based on their daily activities.

## Technology

- Python
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- YouTube Music integration
- AI-powered intent recognition
- SQLite (if enabled for user preferences and memory)

## Project Vision

The vision of MUSIQ AI is to explore how conversational AI can transform the way people discover and interact with music, making music platforms more intuitive and accessible through natural human-language interaction.
